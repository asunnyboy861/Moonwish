Getting started

How do I create my first audio?

Tap "Create my first wish" on the opening screen. Choose a life area, say or type one wish, tap through the suggested chips, then tap "Generate my audio". The script appears line by line, then the audio plays.

Do I need an account?

No. The free tier works without signing in. Cloud backup uses the Apple ID already signed into your device, through your private iCloud database.

Why is there no audio on my device?

If Apple Intelligence is unavailable (iOS below 26 or unsupported hardware), Moonwish uses its on-device script composer so you still get audio. If you hear nothing, check the Ring/Silent switch, the volume, and whether another app is holding the audio route. Profile → Custom API key → Test connection verifies a cloud key if you configured one.

Subscription and billing

What does Plus include?

  • Eight-hour overnight mode with a gentle fade-out timer.
  • All 21 soundscapes and rituals.
  • 30 deep cloud-generated sessions each month.
  • Personal Voice narration, offline downloads, and full export.
  • Vision board with cloud vision support.

How does the 7-day trial work?

The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it converts to $29.99 per year. You can cancel anytime in your App Store subscription settings.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap "Subscriptions".
  4. Select Moonwish.
  5. Choose "Cancel Subscription" or change your plan.

You can also manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Moonwish → Profile → Restore Purchases, or use the Restore Purchases button on the Plus screen.

What is the Cloud Booster?

A $1.99 consumable purchase that adds 30 cloud-generated sessions. It does not expire. Your monthly Plus cloud quota resets on the first of each month.

AI and privacy

Which AI engine is used?

In order: your own API key if configured, Moonwish Cloud if you are on Plus with quota remaining, Apple Intelligence when available, then the built-in on-device composer. The app never leaves you without audio.

Can I use my own API key?

Yes. Open Profile → Custom API key, choose a provider or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and tap Test connection. Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, plus your private CloudKit database if you are signed into iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I export or delete my data?

Profile → Export wishes & audio creates a folder with a JSON manifest and your audio files. To delete a wish, open it in My Channel and choose Delete this wish. To delete everything, remove the app and its iCloud data in Settings.

Sleep and audio

Why does the overnight timer fade out?

The volume ramps down during the final 30 seconds so the audio does not stop abruptly. Choose 30 minutes, 60 minutes, or 8 hours in the player.

Will it keep playing with the screen off?

Yes. Background audio is enabled, and lock-screen controls are available.

What is Personal Voice?

A Plus feature that can narrate affirmations in a voice you create in Settings → Accessibility → Personal Voice. Moonwish asks for authorization only when you turn it on.
