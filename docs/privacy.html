Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Moonwish is designed to be private by default. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and what it never does.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking.
  • No crash-reporting service.
  • No account creation, email sign-up, or password.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers.

Data stored on your device

  • Wishes, chip answers, target dates, session metadata, journal entries, vision board photos, and streak records.
  • Rendered audio files (CAF) and generated soundscape loops, in the app's Application Support directory.
  • Vision board photos you choose, stored locally.
  • Preferences such as notification time, Face ID lock, and Personal Voice selection.

iCloud and CloudKit

Wishes, journal entries, vision board metadata, session metadata, and streak data sync through your private CloudKit database. Only you can access that database. Audio files are kept on the device and are not uploaded to CloudKit. You can disable iCloud sync at the system level; the app continues to work locally.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only when you tap a dictation button to speak a wish or journal entry. Speech recognition is requested from Apple and processed according to your device's Speech settings. We do not store raw audio recordings for dictation.

Photos

Photos are accessed only when you select images for your vision board. They are copied into the app's private storage so the board keeps working offline.

AI processing

  • Apple Intelligence: on supported devices, scripts are generated on-device.
  • Moonwish Cloud (Plus): the wish text, selected life area, and your chip answers are sent to our Cloudflare Worker proxy and then to GLM-5.3-Flash to write the script. Subscription receipts are validated through StoreKit 2.
  • Your own API key: if you configure a custom endpoint, the same wish text and answers are sent directly to the endpoint you choose. The key is stored in the iOS Keychain and is never sent to Moonwish servers.

Generated scripts are returned to your device and saved locally. We do not build a profile of your wishes.

Feedback you send us

If you submit a support ticket, we receive your name, email, selected topic, message, and the app name. We use it only to reply to you. Tickets are handled through our feedback backend at msg.calcs.top.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud database until you delete it. You can delete individual wishes, journal entries, or board images in the app, export your data from Profile, or remove everything by deleting the app and its iCloud data from Settings.

Children

Moonwish is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
